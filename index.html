Highlights

A desktop tool that turns long esports broadcasts into short highlight reels and posts them to the operator's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The operator pastes a broadcast link and types the moments worth keeping, as start and end times.
  2. Highlights cuts those clips, joins them with transitions, and adds a short subscribe card at the end.
  3. With one click, the finished reel is uploaded to the operator's YouTube channel as a private draft and posted to the operator's TikTok account under the same title.

What it connects to

Highlights connects only to accounts owned by the person running it. On TikTok it uses Login Kit to connect the account and the Content Posting API to post the reel. It reads nothing else from the account.

Who it is for

Highlights is an operator's tool for the Esport Highlights channels. It is not a public service and has no sign-up.
