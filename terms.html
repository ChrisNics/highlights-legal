1. What Highlights is

Highlights is a video tool that turns esports broadcasts into short highlight reels. It cuts clips from a recording at times the operator chooses, joins them with transitions, and can upload the finished reel to the operator's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

Highlights runs on the operator's own computer. It is not a hosted service and it does not accept sign-ups from the public. The only accounts it connects to are those belonging to the person running it.

3. Connecting TikTok and YouTube

When you connect an account, the platform asks you to approve specific permissions. Highlights asks only for what it needs:

  • TikTok: read your public profile name, and post videos to your own account.
  • YouTube: upload videos to your own channel.

Highlights never reads your messages, followers, or other people's content. You can disconnect at any time from the platform's own settings, and Highlights will stop posting immediately.

4. What gets posted

Highlights posts only when you start a render with the upload option ticked. Each post carries the title you gave the reel. Posts to TikTok are made with the privacy level you have available; while the app is unreviewed by TikTok, that is "only me". Nothing is posted on a schedule, in the background, or without a render you started.

By posting, you confirm you have the rights to the footage and agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and to each platform's content rules.
